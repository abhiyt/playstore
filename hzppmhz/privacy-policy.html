Privacy Policy: Hz-PPM-Hz

Developer: YTA Tech · Package: com.yta.hzppmhz · Last updated: 8 November 2026

Summary

Hz-PPM-Hz is an offline calculator. It does not collect, store, or share any personal or device data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs that collect data. It does not request any Android permissions and does not access the internet.

How the app works

The frequencies and PPM values you enter are used only for the on-screen calculation, which runs entirely on your device. They are not saved or transmitted. If you tap Copy, the result is placed on your device clipboard. Nothing else happens with it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
