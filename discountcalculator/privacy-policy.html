Privacy Policy: Discount Calculator

Developer: YTA Tech · Package: com.abhishekyt.discountcalculator · Last updated: 8 November 2026

Summary

Discount Calculator is an offline calculator. It does not collect, store, or share any personal or device data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs that collect data. It does not request any Android permissions and does not access the internet.

How the app works

Prices and percentages you enter are used only for the on-screen calculation, which runs entirely on your device. If you tap Save, that result is stored in the app's private storage on your phone so it appears in History; it never leaves the device and is deleted when you tap Clear all or uninstall the app. If you tap Share, the result text is handed to the app you choose.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
