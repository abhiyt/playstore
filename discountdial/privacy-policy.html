Privacy Policy: Discount Dial

Developer: YTA Tech · Package: com.yta.discountcalculator · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Summary

Discount Dial is a shopping calculator. Your calculations stay on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Your calculations

Prices, discounts and tax rates you enter are processed only on your device. If you tap Save, the result is stored in the app's private storage on your phone so it appears in History. It is never sent to us, and is deleted when you tap Clear all or uninstall the app. If you tap Share, the result text is passed to the app you choose. Your settings (currency, decimal places, theme) are also stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To serve, personalise (where allowed) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance information

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the app's menu under Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

Information we collect

We (YTA Tech) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, sign-in or analytics of our own.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
