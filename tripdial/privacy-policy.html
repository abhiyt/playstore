Privacy Policy: TripDial

Developer: YTA Tech · Package: com.yta.tripdial · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Summary

TripDial is a trip expense tracker. Your trips, travellers and expenses are stored on your device, and we have no servers that receive them. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Your trips and expenses

Trip names, dates, traveller names, expenses, amounts, categories and splits that you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never sent to us. Your settings (theme and backup choice) are also stored only on your device. Deleting a trip or expense removes it from the app, and uninstalling the app deletes all of this data from your phone.

Traveller names are whatever you type in. TripDial does not access your contacts.

Sharing

When you tap Share, TripDial creates a summary message or PDF report on your device and hands it to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp or email. What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Backup and restore

If you use Back up now or Auto backup, TripDial writes a backup file of your trips to the location you pick in Android's file picker, for example Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. The file goes directly from your device to that service, under your own account and that service's privacy policy. We never receive it. Restore reads a backup file you choose. You can stop auto backup in Settings at any time, and delete backup files from your cloud storage yourself.

Advertising

The free version shows banner and full-screen ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To serve, personalise (where allowed) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance information

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your trip data is never shared with advertisers.

If you are in a region that requires it, such as the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

TripDial Pro users do not see ads.

Purchases

TripDial Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. We never see your card, UPI or other payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether your Google account owns TripDial Pro, so it can unlock Pro features and restore your purchase on a new phone.

Information we collect

We (YTA Tech) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, sign-in or analytics of our own. It uses the internet only for ads, Google Play purchases, and backups you choose to make.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
